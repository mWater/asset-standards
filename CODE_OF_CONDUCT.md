# Code of conduct

This repository is a working space for people who manage water and sanitation
infrastructure data. Discussion should stay useful to that work.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to be courteous, to keep criticism directed at the
standard rather than at the person proposing a change, and to assume that a
colleague reporting a defect is trying to help. Many participants write in a second
or third language, and a clumsy sentence is not a slight.

Disagreement about a technical question is normal and welcome. Governments,
utilities and non-profits work under different constraints, and a proposal that
suits one may not suit another. Say which constraint is driving a position.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following will be removed and may lead to a participant being blocked:
harassment or personal attacks; discriminatory remarks about any person or group;
publishing another person's private information; sustained disruption of a
discussion; and sexual language or imagery.

Two further points reflect the sector this repository serves. Do not post personal
data about the people served by a water or sanitation system, including names,
telephone numbers, household locations or photographs of identifiable people. Use a
synthetic example instead. Do not use issues to attack a named government official
or a named utility employee; report the technical problem without the person.

## Reporting

Report a concern to conduct@mwater.co. Reports go to mWater's Head of Product and
COO, and stay confidential to those two unless the reporter agrees otherwise.

mWater will acknowledge a report within five working days and say what action, if
any, it is taking. Possible actions are a private warning, removal of the content,
and blocking the participant from the repository. A participant who is blocked may
appeal in writing to the same address.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other activity in
this repository, and to communication about the repository in other venues where a
participant is representing the project.
