# Code of conduct

This repository is a working space for people who manage water and sanitation
infrastructure data. Discussion should stay useful to that work.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to be courteous, to direct criticism at the standard rather
than at the person proposing a change, and to assume that a colleague reporting a
defect is trying to help. Many participants write in a second or third language, and
a clumsy sentence is not a slight.

Disagreement about a technical question is normal and welcome. Governments,
utilities and non-profits work under different constraints, and a proposal that suits
one may not suit another. Say which constraint is driving a position.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following will be removed and may lead to a participant being blocked:
harassment or personal attacks; discriminatory remarks about any person or group;
publishing another person's private information; sustained disruption of a
discussion; and sexual language or imagery.

Two further points reflect the sector this repository serves. Do not post personal
data about the people served by a water or sanitation system, including names,
telephone numbers, household locations or photographs of identifiable people. Use a
synthetic example instead. Do not use issues to attack a named government official or
a named utility employee. Report the technical problem without the person.

## Reporting

Report a concern to Petri Autio, Head of Product, at petri@mwater.co. Reports are
kept confidential unless the reporter agrees otherwise. mWater is a small team and
does not promise a response time, but every report is read.

Possible actions are a private warning, removal of the content, and blocking the
participant from the repository.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other activity in
this repository.
